NEED TO KNOW

Technology FAQs

Are all Alberto watches water resistant?

Ratings vary by model. Check the product specifications and water-resistance rating before exposing your watch to water.

What's the difference between automatic and quartz?

Automatic watches use a mechanical movement, while quartz watches use a battery-powered electronic movement.

Do all watches have smart features?

No. Smart features are available only on selected smart watch models; traditional collections focus on classic timekeeping.

Where can I compare watch specifications?

Visit our product collection to explore different styles and review the details listed for each timepiece.
